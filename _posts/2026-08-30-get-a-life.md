---
title: "Get a Life"
author: Sergio E. Betancourt
category: poetry
layout: post
---

Shrill song awakens all.

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Hammering, yappering,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">click here, clank there.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">It's early morning and</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">the crews show such diligence,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">such fury,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">to change everything</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">that mother Earth</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">and father time</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">came together to make.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Thankfully the assault ends at five</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">or four if it's a Friday.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">People and fauna will smile</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">to themselves, to each other,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">ignorant of the plans</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">brought forth to</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">extend the project</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">by another decade or two.</p>
