---
title: "What Treasures We Find"
author: Sergio E. Betancourt
category: poetry
layout: post
---

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Broken bottle, I see you.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">I left home this morning,</p> 

<p style="text-indent: 0;">desperate to clear my mind,</p> 

<p style="text-indent: 0;">but instead I saw you,</p> 

<p style="text-indent: 0;">forgotten yet ever-present.</p> 

<br>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">You were on a bridge,</p>

sun beating down on your films and edges.

I swear you were colorless, 

but all-powerful, effortlessly refracting light;

at times burning

living things that dared cross your path, 

at times revealing the technicolor 

<p style="text-indent: 0;">that fuels all the world’s beauty.</p>

<br>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Step by step, I meant to forget</p>

my woes when I left home. 

Step by step, an invisible thread guided me to you. 

I failed to notice you at first, 

until I harmed you. 

The crunching of your bits alerted me.

I first thought I stepped on a shrew,

maybe a handful of pebbles, but 

how wrong I was. I looked 

down to assess the damage to my shoe. 

Excuse me for not thinking of you. 

Thinking no damage was done,

I immediately noticed you, 

bleeding, resting, waiting. 

Your label looked 

partially scratched off, 

the remaining ink and paper discolored and diffused, 

<p style="text-indent: 0;">your identity intentionally erased.</p>

<br>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Broken bottle, I noticed your edges and lines,</p>

thinking those had been there

from the dawn of time, 

from the moment you came to existence,

whether you knew it or not. These edges and lines,

finally revealed to yourself and others 

by tragedy or celebration, conflict or necessity. 

These sharp adornments, 

endowed with the ability to cut 

and puncture–they aren’t yours anymore. 

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Perhaps they never were.</p>

<br>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">I wondered what you held.</p> 

I dared not taste it. 

<p style="text-indent: 0;">I wondered what you went through.</p> 

I dared not imagine it. 

<p style="text-indent: 0;">I wondered who made you, but it never mattered.</p>  

<br>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">You were there and so was I.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">You’re still with me,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">long after someone, something,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">or nothing at all,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">disposed of you.</p>
