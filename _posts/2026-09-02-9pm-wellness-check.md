What took you so long?

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Sleek, sharp-eyed, you</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">interrupt me,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Each step you take is</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">gilded in elegance, a</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">gift from those before.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">My writing ceases to matter.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">My speech is cut short.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">You request eyes on you</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">and really nothing more.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Did you ever wonder</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">if I'm meant to be abhorred?</p>
