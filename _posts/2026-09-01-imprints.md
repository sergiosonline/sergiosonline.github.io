---
title: "Imprints"
author: Sergio E. Betancourt
category: poetry
layout: post
---

Ink, blood, spit,

<p style="text-indent: .5;">any liquid will do.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Hand on face, I wonder</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">how can one hurt so easily.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">You told me you'd love me,</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">despite moods and shortcomings,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">yet I feel this above all else.</p>

<br>


<p style="text-indent: 0;">Now, without a choice, reduced to</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">what you dare look at,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">but not touch,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0.5;">and what you dare touch,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">but not look at,</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">I dream of what shall come to be.</p>


<br>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Once we're gone, long after our entrails</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">sour and dry up, long after our bones</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">erode and crumble, your imprint</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">will live on, everlasting, eternal.</p>
