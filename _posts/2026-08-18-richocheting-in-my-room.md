---
title: "Ricocheting in My Room"
author: Sergio E. Betancourt
category: poetry
layout: post
---

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Bright, violent sunlight</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">enters my bedroom.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Unfettered, unbothered, it</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">illuminates my crusty eyes</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">and my unshaven face.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Energized I stand</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">and spin around.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Tired, hopeless, I crash.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">First against the nightstand.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Then against the dresser.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">I end up on the floor,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">bruised and bloodied,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">wishing I was sunlight,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">and nothing more.</p>
