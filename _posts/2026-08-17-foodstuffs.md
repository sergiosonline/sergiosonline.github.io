---
title: "Foodstuffs"
author: Sergio E. Betancourt
category: poetry
layout: post
---

I opened my mouth like last time:

<p style="text-indent: .5;">jaws first; teeth, tongue, lips following.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Synchronized, highly complex reaction</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">to the succulent stew in front.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Where would I be today if</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">I hadn't opened my mouth?</p>

<br>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Back then,</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">with a juice-covered tongue,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">grease-soaked teeth,</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">and gunk-cradled gums,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">words came out of me,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0.5;">effortlessly tangled with chewing noises.</p>

<br>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">In that moment I spilled,</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">spilled so many secrets,</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">things only for me to know.</p>

<br>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">But I faced needs,</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">primordial,</p>

<p style="text-indent: .5;">everlasting.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0;">Now I dare wonder if</p>

<p style="text-indent: 0.5;">I'll ever open my mouth again.</p>
